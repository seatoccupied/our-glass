OUR GLASS
=========

A tiny incremental game about very small men and one very large hourglass.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Double-click index.html — it opens in your browser. That's the whole install.
3. Sandmen rain in. Landings earn sand. Spend sand on the left.
4. Fill the bottom chamber to 100%, then hit the big FLIP button.
   Everything they built... well. You'll see. They're fine. They're always fine.
5. Scroll to zoom in on the little guys. Drag to pan. Double-click to reset.
6. Click once anywhere to turn the sound on (browser rule). Sliders bottom-left.

The game saves itself in your browser automatically. Export/Import (bottom of
the panel) moves a save between machines as a text blob.
